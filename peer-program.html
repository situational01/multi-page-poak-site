Peer Program

Growing confidence through peer support.

Boys for Boys and Girls for Girls create safe spaces where young people can learn, support one another, build confidence and develop practical skills for life.

Explore the programme Support a young person
About the programme

Safe spaces where young people can grow together.

POAK's Peer Program creates supportive spaces where girls and boys can talk openly, learn from one another and develop the confidence to navigate challenges.

Through Girls for Girls and Boys for Boys initiatives, the programme addresses menstrual health, mental wellbeing, social pressure, relationships, confidence and positive role models.

By strengthening peer relationships and practical life skills, young people become better equipped to support themselves and contribute positively to their schools, families and communities.

Programme focus

Peer support, mentorship, menstrual health, life skills, peer counselling, positive role models and stronger support systems for young people.

What we offer

Practical support for young people.

The programme combines peer-led learning, mentorship and practical support to help young people build confidence, resilience and skills for everyday life.

01

Sanitary Pad Distribution

Providing menstrual products to girls facing barriers to accessing them, helping them manage their periods with dignity and remain engaged in school.

02

Menstrual Health Education

Helping girls understand menstruation, hygiene, self-care and the importance of reliable health information.

03

Boys Mentorship Circles

Supportive spaces where boys discuss responsibility, confidence, emotional wellbeing, respectful relationships and positive decision-making.

04

Peer Counselling Training

Equipping selected young people with listening, communication and peer-support skills, including when to involve a trusted adult or professional.

05

Life Skills Workshops

Building communication, self-esteem, decision-making, teamwork, responsibility and future-planning skills.

06

School & Community Partnerships

Working with schools, families and community organisations to strengthen support systems for young people.

Why this programme matters

Young people need spaces where they can be heard.

Adolescence brings important changes and difficult decisions. Young people need trusted people they can talk to, practical information and positive examples.

Peer support complements families, schools and professionals by creating opportunities for young people to learn together and build supportive relationships.

Dignity Menstrual health information and access to essential menstrual products.
Confidence Opportunities to participate, lead and develop stronger self-confidence.
Belonging Supportive peer environments where young people can learn and feel connected.
Programme impact

Building stronger young people through connection.

POAK's Peer Program brings together mentorship, peer support and practical education to help young people develop skills and relationships that extend beyond the programme.
