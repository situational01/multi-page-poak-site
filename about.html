About POAK

Fighting poverty in Kenya since 7 December 1997.

Our story

POAK, short for Poverty Africa Kenya, was founded on 7 December 1997 by a group of community leaders who saw the need to address poverty where they lived.

What began as a grassroots initiative has grown into an organisation working with communities across eight counties in Kenya. Our approach has stayed grounded in a simple principle: lasting change begins when communities help shape their own future.

Over the years, POAK has worked with more than 5,000 Kenyans across over 120 communities, through work in youth digital skills, women's economic empowerment, maternal and child health, mental health and community development.

Est. 1997 More than 28 years working alongside Kenyan communities.

A message from our co-founder

"When we started POAK in 1997, we had a simple belief: that poverty is not inevitable, but a challenge that can be overcome when communities come together. Today, after 28 years, I am prouder than ever of what we've built together. We've seen women start businesses, young people find meaningful work, mothers get access to healthcare, and communities take ownership of their own development. Our work is far from done, but every day we renew our commitment to a Kenya free from poverty."

Eng. Edwin Liyai

Co-founder

Mission & vision

Our mission

To help end poverty in Kenya, and to bring every part of society into that work, so that living standards rise for Kenyans everywhere.

Our vision

A Kenya free from poverty, where everyone has an equal chance to thrive, and where communities lead their own sustainable development.

Core values

  • Equity & inclusion

    Every Kenyan deserves an equal chance, regardless of background.

  • Community empowerment

    We put communities at the centre of the work we do.

  • Integrity & accountability

    We hold ourselves to transparency and account for what we do.

  • Partnership & collaboration

    We work alongside government, NGOs, partners and communities to make change last.

  • Sustainability

Our journey

For more than 28 years, POAK has worked alongside communities to create opportunities and respond to changing needs. Our journey has included growth, transition and renewal, always grounded in community development.

  • 1997

    The beginning

    POAK was founded on 7 December 1997, with a commitment to addressing poverty and supporting underserved communities.

  • 1999

    Supporting families

    In its early years, POAK supported families through in-kind donations while building relationships within the community.

  • 2000

    Growing into the community

    POAK extended its work beyond its initial activities, with a stronger focus on community development and local capacity.

  • 2001

    Expanding our reach

    The organisation expanded its work to additional communities and counties, strengthening its presence across Kenya.

  • 2006

    Improving learning spaces

    POAK supported classroom renovation aimed at creating better learning environments for children.

  • 2006–2025

    A period of transition

    POAK went through a period of dormancy as the organisation underwent significant changes before beginning a new chapter.

  • 2026

    A new chapter

    POAK returned with renewed energy and a broader focus on the changing needs of communities across Kenya.
