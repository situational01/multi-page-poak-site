About POAK

Fighting poverty in Kenya since 7th December 1997

Our Story

POAK (Poverty Africa - Kenya) was founded on December 7th, 1997 by a group of community leaders who saw the urgent need to address poverty in their local communities. What started as a small grassroots initiative has grown into a recognized nonprofit organization serving communities across 8 counties in Kenya.

For over 28 years, we have remained true to our founding principle: that sustainable change comes from empowering communities to lead their own development. Our programs are designed and implemented with direct input from the people we serve.

Today, POAK has empowered over 5,000 Kenyans, reached 120+ communities, and established programs in youth digital skills, women's economic empowerment, maternal and child health, and mental health wellness.

Our Journey

Poverty Africa Kenya has been working with communities for over 28 years. Since 1997, POAK has empowered children, youth, women, and vulnerable communities through sustainable development programs across Kenya.

1997

The Beginning

POAK was founded on 7th December 1997 with a mission to alleviate poverty and empower underserved communities through grassroots initiatives.

1999

Inkind donations

POAK was initially focused on inkind donations to support the founding members and their families.

2000

Expanding into the Community

POAK began to expand its reach into the broader community, focusing on sustainable development and local capacity building.

2001

Expanding to Different Counties

POAK expanded its operations to different counties in Kenya, establishing a stronger presence and impact in various communities.

2006

Classroom Renovation

POAK invested in renovating classrooms to provide a better learning environment for students.

2006-2025

The organization went dormant

The organization went dormant for several years, with no active programs or initiatives.

2026

Rebirth of POAK

POAK was reborn with a renewed mission to address the evolving needs of the communities it serves.
