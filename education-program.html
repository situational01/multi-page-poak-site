Education Programme

Connecting young people to opportunity.

POAK connects young people to digital skills, scholarships, mentorship and education opportunities through trusted partners.

Explore the programme Partner with POAK
Our approach

Connecting learners to the right opportunities.

POAK is developing a network of universities, colleges, coding bootcamps, training institutions and facilitators.

Through these partnerships, young people can access relevant learning, digital skills, scholarships and career pathways based on their interests and goals.

Programme status

Our education partner network is currently being developed. Opportunities will be shared as partnerships are formally established.

How it works

From opportunity to action.

We make it easier for young people to find, understand and access credible opportunities.

01

Find opportunities

Identify relevant education, skills and scholarship opportunities.

02

Vet partners

Work with credible institutions and experienced facilitators.

03

Connect learners

Match young people with opportunities aligned with their goals.

04

Follow up

Provide guidance and support throughout the learner's journey.

Opportunities

What young people can gain

As the network grows, learners will be connected to opportunities that strengthen their skills, confidence and career prospects.

  • Digital skills training through selected partners.
  • Scholarships and sponsorships for eligible learners.
  • Career guidance for education and career decisions.
  • Internship pathways where opportunities are available.
  • Mentorship and application support throughout the process.

Areas of opportunity

  • Industry-relevant digital skills
  • Coding and technology
  • Professional and technical education
  • Scholarships and funded learning
  • Internships and workplace exposure
  • Career mentorship
