WHO WE ARE

Real change takes time and local commitment.

POAK (Poverty Africa – Kenya) is a community-centered nonprofit working with communities to address poverty through practical, sustainable solutions. Our work connects education, wellbeing, economic opportunity and environmental resilience.

Founded in 1997, our approach is built on collaboration, local knowledge, and the belief that communities should lead their own progress.

OUR WORK

What We Do

Four connected areas of work, one shared goal: stronger, healthier and more resilient communities.

01 / Learning and livelihoods

Education and Economic Empowerment

Supporting access to education, practical skills and economic opportunities that strengthen livelihoods.

02 / Health and care

Mental Health and Wellbeing

Promoting mental health, nutrition and supportive spaces for healthier individuals and communities.

03 / Sustainable agriculture

Smart Agriculture and Food Security

Building farming skills and sustainable food systems to improve nutrition, food security and livelihoods.

04 / Communities and climate

Climate Resilience

Helping communities adapt to climate challenges through environmental conservation and practical resilience initiatives.
