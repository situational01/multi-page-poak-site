Youth Digital Skills

Building a network to connect youth with quality digital training

Our Model

We partner, we don't train directly

POAK Kenya does not offer in‑house digital skills training. Instead, we are actively building a network of trusted institutions, coding bootcamps, universities, colleges, and experienced facilitators who will deliver high‑quality, market‑ready programs. Once partnerships are established, our role will be to identify opportunities, vet partners for quality, and connect youth to the right training paths—often with scholarships or subsidized slots.

How It Will Work

  • Curated partner network – We will work only with trusted training providers who have proven track records.
  • Scholarship & sponsorship links – We will help eligible youth access funded programs.
  • Career support – Many partners will offer internships, job placement, and industry connections.
  • Mentorship & guidance – POAK will provide pre‑enrollment counseling, application help, and follow‑up mentoring.

Partner Network – In Development

We are currently actively seeking partners – universities, technical colleges, bootcamps, and independent facilitators – who share our mission of empowering youth through digital skills.

If you represent a training institution or are a skilled facilitator, we’d love to hear from you. Reach out to us to explore partnership opportunities.

What Learners Will Gain (Through Future Partners)

  • Industry‑recognized certifications
  • Internship and job placement assistance
  • Access to hiring partners and alumni networks
  • Career mentorship and portfolio building
