Mental Health & Nutrition Programme

Healthier minds. Stronger communities.

POAK works to strengthen mental wellbeing and physical health through mental health awareness, counselling, peer support, nutrition education and community-based wellness initiatives.

Explore the programme Partner with POAK
Our approach

Supporting healthier minds and bodies.

POAK's Mental Health & Nutrition Programme combines mental health awareness, psychosocial support and practical nutrition education to help individuals and families build healthier lives.

Through workshops, peer support, counselling, nutrition education and referrals, POAK makes information and support more accessible within vulnerable communities.

Programme focus

Mental health awareness, psychosocial support, counselling, peer support, nutrition education, wellness promotion and connections to appropriate professional services.

What we do

Practical support for wellbeing.

We combine awareness, community support and practical education to help individuals and families make informed decisions about their wellbeing.

01

Mental Health Workshops

Community and school sessions that promote mental health awareness, reduce stigma and encourage healthy ways of responding to everyday challenges.

02

Peer Support

Safe spaces where young people, caregivers and community members can share experiences and build supportive relationships.

03

Counselling & Referral

Support and appropriate referrals for people who may require additional professional or healthcare services.

04

Nutrition & Wellness

Practical nutrition education covering healthy food choices, meal planning, food budgeting and everyday family wellness.

Our approach

Turning awareness into practical support.

POAK moves beyond awareness by connecting communities with practical knowledge, supportive spaces and appropriate services.

Community participation helps ensure activities respond to local needs and remain accessible.

01

Raise Awareness

Community and school sessions on mental health, nutrition and wellbeing.

02

Build Community Capacity

Strengthening the skills of peer supporters, facilitators and community actors.

03

Create Safe Spaces

Supportive environments where people can learn, participate and seek help.

04

Connect to Care

Connecting participants with qualified professionals and relevant services.
