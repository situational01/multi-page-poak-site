Mental Health & Nutrition Wellness

Healing minds and bodies through integrated mental health support and nutrition education

About the Program

In Kenya, mental health challenges affect 1 in 4 people, yet only 1 in 10 receive care. At the same time, malnutrition and food insecurity worsen mental well‑being. POAK's Mental Health & Nutrition Wellness program addresses this link by combining professional mental health support with practical nutrition education. We believe that a healthy mind and a well‑nourished body are foundations for escaping poverty.

Our holistic approach provides counseling, peer support groups, and nutrition workshops – all free of charge – to vulnerable communities across Kenya.

What We Offer

Mental Health Workshops

Interactive workshops in schools and communities to raise awareness, reduce stigma, and teach coping skills for stress, anxiety, and depression.

Support Groups

Safe, confidential spaces for women, youth, and caregivers to share experiences and support each other through life’s challenges.

Professional Counseling

Individual and family counseling for anxiety, depression, trauma, grief, and relationship issues – delivered by trained counselors.

Nutrition & Wellness

Hands‑on nutrition education, cooking demonstrations, and food budgeting skills to improve physical health and mental resilience.

Our Approach

1

Raise Awareness

Community workshops on mental health and nutrition, breaking stigma and encouraging help‑seeking behaviour.

2

Train Facilitators

Train peer supporters and community health volunteers to deliver both mental health first aid and basic nutrition advice.

3

Create Safe Spaces

Establish regular support groups and nutrition circles in accessible community locations.

4

Connect to Care

Provide referrals to professional counselors, healthcare providers, and food security programmes.
