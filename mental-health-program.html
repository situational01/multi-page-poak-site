Mental Health & Wellness

Healing minds, transforming lives through community-based care

About the Program

In Kenya, mental health challenges affect 1 in 4 people, yet only 1 in 10 receive the care they need. Stigma, lack of awareness, and limited access to services prevent many from seeking help. POAK's Mental Health & Wellness program provides community-based mental health support through trained counselors, peer support groups, and therapeutic interventions.

We believe that mental health is fundamental to overall well-being and poverty alleviation. Our holistic approach combines professional counseling with community support systems to create safe spaces for healing and growth.

What We Offer

Mental Health Workshops

Interactive workshops in schools, workplaces, and communities to raise awareness, reduce stigma, and teach coping skills. Topics include stress management, anxiety, depression, and emotional resilience.

Support Groups

Safe, confidential spaces where individuals can share experiences, find solidarity, and support each other through challenges. Groups include women's circles, youth groups, and caregivers support.

Professional Counseling

Individual and family counseling sessions with trained counselors. We address issues including anxiety, depression, trauma, grief, and relationship challenges.

Group Therapy

Facilitated therapeutic sessions where participants learn coping strategies, build social connections, and work through shared experiences in a supportive environment.

Our Approach

1

Raise Awareness

Community workshops to educate about mental health, reduce stigma, and encourage help-seeking behavior.

2

Train Facilitators

Train community members as peer supporters and group facilitators to ensure sustainable, locally-led support.

3

Create Safe Spaces

Establish regular support groups and therapy sessions in accessible community locations.

4

Connect to Care

Provide referrals to professional counselors and healthcare providers for specialized support.
