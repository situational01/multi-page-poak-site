Girls for Girls

Keeping girls in school through sanitary pads and mental health support

About the Program

In Kenya, 1 in 10 girls miss school during their period due to lack of access to sanitary pads. Many drop out entirely. The Girls for Girls program addresses this crisis by providing sanitary pads and mental health workshops to keep girls in school and thriving.

We believe that every girl deserves to attend school with dignity and confidence. Our program creates safe spaces where girls can learn, share, and grow together.

What We Offer

  • Sanitary Pad Distribution - Monthly supply of reusable or disposable pads to keep girls in school
  • Menstrual Health Education - Teaching girls about their bodies and menstrual hygiene
  • Mental Health Workshops - Building confidence, self-esteem, and emotional resilience
  • Peer Support Groups - Creating safe spaces for girls to share experiences
  • School Partnerships - Working with schools to identify and support vulnerable girls
  • Parental Engagement - Involving families to create supportive home environments
