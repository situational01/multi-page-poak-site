4K Clubs for Schools & Institutions

Growing future agricultural leaders.

Practical agricultural learning that helps young people discover farming as a pathway to skills, innovation, entrepreneurship and sustainable livelihoods.

Explore the programme
Our Programme

Making agriculture practical, relevant and inspiring.

Agriculture remains an important part of Kenya's economy, yet many young people do not see farming as a viable career or source of opportunity.

POAK's 4K Clubs programme introduces learners to practical agriculture, smart farming, environmental stewardship and entrepreneurship from an early age.

Through hands-on learning, mentorship and school-based projects, learners develop skills that can contribute to stronger livelihoods, food security and climate-resilient communities.

Long-term vision

To inspire a generation of young agricultural leaders who transform knowledge into sustainable livelihoods, strengthen food security and drive climate-resilient development.

Why this programme matters

Learning agriculture beyond the classroom.

The programme combines agricultural knowledge with practical experience, leadership and entrepreneurship.

Learners also develop environmental awareness, nutrition knowledge, teamwork and financial literacy.

Practical Skills Hands-on experience through school gardens, demonstration farms and agricultural projects.
Innovation Introducing learners to smart farming, technology and sustainable production.
Leadership Building responsibility, teamwork, entrepreneurship and leadership skills.

Long-Term Impact

Growing a generation ready to lead Kenya's agricultural future.

POAK aims to help young people see agriculture not simply as farming, but as an opportunity for innovation, enterprise, environmental stewardship and sustainable livelihoods. By starting early, the programme can help build stronger agricultural communities for the future.
