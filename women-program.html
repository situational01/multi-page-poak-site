Women's Economic Empowerment

Building a future where every woman can thrive economically

In Development

Program Vision

POAK is developing a comprehensive Women's Economic Empowerment program to help women build financial independence. While we are not yet offering these services, we are actively planning to launch using the proven Village Savings and Loan Association (VSLA) model. Women will form groups, pool savings, and provide small loans to each other to start or expand businesses.

Planned Offerings

  • VSLA Groups – Women saving together and accessing small loans (planned)
  • Business Training – Financial literacy, marketing, and business planning (planned)
  • Startup Support – Seed capital and equipment for new businesses (planned)
  • Market Linkages – Connecting women to markets and customers (planned)
  • Leadership Development – Training women to lead and mentor others (planned)

How VSLA Will Work

1

Form a Group

15-25 women meet weekly, each contributing a small amount to a shared fund.

2

Save Together

Savings accumulate over 9-12 months, typically 100-500 KES per week.

3

Access Loans

Members can borrow from the group fund at low interest.

4

Share Profits

At year end, profits are shared equally among all members.
