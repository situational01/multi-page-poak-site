4K Smart Farming

Building resilient farming communities.

Climate change, rising input costs, water scarcity and limited market access continue to affect smallholder farmers across Kenya.

POAK's 4K Smart Farming programme provides practical, climate-smart solutions that improve food security, strengthen household incomes and help farming communities adapt to changing conditions.

Programme goal

To promote practical climate-smart agriculture, strengthen household livelihoods and build sustainable and resilient farming communities.

Why this programme matters

Helping farmers adapt, produce and prosper.

Smallholder farmers face increasing pressure from unpredictable weather, water shortages, expensive agricultural inputs and weak market access.

The programme connects farmers with practical knowledge, appropriate technologies and opportunities to strengthen both production and household income.

Food Security Supporting households to produce nutritious food more reliably throughout changing seasons.
Resilient Livelihoods Improving farming practices and income opportunities for vulnerable households.
Climate Adaptation Promoting farming approaches that use water, land and other resources more efficiently.
Market Access Helping farmers strengthen market connections, cooperation and opportunities for better returns.

Programme highlights

Starting with communities where the need is greatest.

POAK has identified communities for the initial implementation phase. These locations will serve as pilot sites for demonstrating climate-smart farming approaches that can be adapted and replicated in other regions.

  • Isiolo County
  • Congo Community – Ruiru

Pilot approach

The programme will establish practical demonstration sites where farmers can learn, test and adopt climate-smart farming methods suited to local conditions.

Long-term impact

Stronger farms. Stronger households. Resilient communities.

The programme aims to build a generation of farmers with practical agricultural skills, strengthen household food security and incomes, promote climate-smart farming and create sustainable livelihoods that can withstand environmental and economic pressures.
