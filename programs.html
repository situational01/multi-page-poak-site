Peer Program

Boys for Boys & Girls for Girls – creating safe spaces, mentorship, and life skills training. We address period poverty for girls and provide mental wellness support for both genders through peer‑led groups.

  • Sanitary Pad Distribution
  • Menstrual Health Education
  • Boys Mentorship Circles
  • Peer Counseling Training

Mental Health & Nutrition

Holistic wellness combining mental health support and nutrition education. We offer maternal mental health screening, youth peer support groups, and community‑based nutrition workshops.

  • Maternal Mental Health Screening
  • Youth Peer Support Groups
  • Nutrition Education Workshops
  • CHV Training on Mental Health

Why Our Programs Work

Community-Led

All programs are designed with input from the communities we serve, ensuring relevance and sustainability.

Data-Driven

We continuously monitor and evaluate our programs to ensure maximum impact and effectiveness.

Partnership-Based

We work with local governments, businesses, and other NGOs to amplify our impact.

Sustainable

Our programs build local capacity and create lasting change that continues long after we leave.
