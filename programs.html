Providing sanitary pads and mental health workshops to keep girls in school. We address period poverty and provide safe spaces for girls to learn, grow, and thrive.

Why Our Programs Work

Community-Led

All programs are designed with input from the communities we serve, ensuring relevance and sustainability.

Data-Driven

We continuously monitor and evaluate our programs to ensure maximum impact and effectiveness.

Partnership-Based

We work with local governments, businesses, and other NGOs to amplify our impact.

Sustainable

Our programs build local capacity and create lasting change that continues long after we leave.
