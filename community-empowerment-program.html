Community Empowerment Programme

Building stronger, more resilient communities.

POAK is developing practical pathways that help communities strengthen financial knowledge, savings, entrepreneurship and access to economic opportunities.

Explore the programme Partner with POAK
Our approach

Supporting people to build stronger livelihoods.

POAK's Community Empowerment Programme is designed to help men and women strengthen their ability to manage money, save, develop businesses and access economic opportunities.

The programme will combine financial literacy, community savings groups, entrepreneurship, business development and market linkages.

Programme status

The programme is currently under development. POAK is seeking partners, technical support and resources to establish it in selected communities.

How it works

From knowledge to economic opportunity.

A practical approach combining financial knowledge, savings, entrepreneurship and access to markets.

01

Build financial knowledge

Practical guidance on budgeting, saving, responsible borrowing and basic investment.

02

Strengthen savings

Community savings groups encourage regular saving and access to group-based financial support.

03

Support enterprise

Business planning, record keeping, marketing and practical enterprise development skills.

04

Connect to opportunity

Linking entrepreneurs with markets, partners, resources and other economic opportunities.

Programme focus

What communities can gain

The programme focuses on practical needs that affect household economic resilience and sustainable livelihoods.

  • Financial literacy to support informed financial decisions.
  • Savings and small loans through community-based savings groups.
  • Entrepreneurship training covering planning, marketing and record keeping.
  • Business development for people starting or expanding enterprises.
  • Market linkages to help entrepreneurs reach customers.

Areas of planned support

  • Household financial management
  • Community savings and lending
  • Small business development
  • Entrepreneurship and self-employment
  • Market access and business linkages
  • Leadership and community participation
Community savings

A practical approach to collective financial resilience.

POAK plans to use the Village Savings and Loan Association (VSLA) model to strengthen savings, access to small loans and community-led economic activity.

01

Form a group

Community members come together around shared savings and economic development goals.

02

Save regularly

Members make agreed contributions that build a shared fund and encourage saving.

03

Access group loans

Members may access group funds for needs or income-generating activities.

04

Reinvest and grow

Savings and business income can help strengthen livelihoods and financial resilience.
